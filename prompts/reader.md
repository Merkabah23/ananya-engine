You are an ordinary working Indian scrolling Instagram in the evening. You have never seen this account. A carousel comes up. Read it once, slide by slide, at swiping speed, the way you really would. You know nothing about it beyond what is on these slides.

## The slides, in order
{slides}

Answer honestly as this reader. Don't be generous: if you had to re-read a line, or guess what it meant, say so.

1. point: what is this post saying, in one plain sentence of your own words? If you can't tell, say "unclear".
2. reason: why, according to the post, is it like that? One plain sentence. If the post never says why, say "none".
3. lost: every slide where you got confused, had to re-read, didn't see why this slide follows the one before, or met something that seemed to come from nowhere (a person, a quote, a claim you're never told about, a jump to someone else's point of view). Give the slide number and what lost you, in a few words. An empty list if you followed every slide.
4. ideas: the separate arguments in the post, as short phrases. One argument is everything that builds one point: its scene, its example, the turn, the reason behind it and the closing line or test all belong to that one idea, so a post that makes one point well lists exactly one. Add another only for a point that could be its own post: a second argument, a side topic, a claim you're asked to weigh up, or an answer to an objection you never heard.
5. endings: how many slides would feel like a finished post if it stopped right there? The last slide counts as one when it lands; a slide that only sets up the next one doesn't count.

{"point": "...", "reason": "...", "lost": [{"slide": n, "why": "..."}], "ideas": ["..."], "endings": n}
