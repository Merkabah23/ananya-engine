## The shape
1. line: the post's thought said in a new way, in under 110 characters. It is the first thing a reader sees under the cover, so it must not repeat slide 1; it gives them the turn, the reversal or the rule from the post, said the way he would say it out loud. Plain words, no question, no hashtag, no emoji. It names the thing the post is about in the words a person would type into Instagram search (shagun envelope, health insurance, bank RM, house help, Diwali release), naturally, inside the sentence.
2. body: zero, one or two short lines (under 140 characters each) that add one thing the line didn't: the because, or the real line someone says (Hinglish is fine). Never a summary of the slides, never a new fact, figure, brand or claim that isn't already in the slides.
3. ask: exactly one, chosen for this post:
   - "send" (the default): name the exact person the reader will think of and why it's them, like the writer's send line above (you may reuse or sharpen it). Starts with "Send this to".
   - "question": only when the answer is a word or a short personal answer anyone can type in two seconds ("Eldest or youngest?", "What did yours call it?"). Never "what do you think?" or "agree?".
   - "save": only when the post gives the reader something to check or do later. Starts with "Save this for".
4. search_phrase: the 2-4 word phrase from line or body that someone would search for. It must appear word for word in line or body.
5. topic_tag: one hashtag, CamelCase, for exactly what this post is about, that a person would actually search or follow (#HealthInsurance, #Shagun, #HouseHelp, #BankRM). Not a feeling, not a pun, not a broad tag (#Life, #India, #Viral, #Trending, #Motivation, #Explore, #Reels, #Instagood, #FYP).

## Rules
- Second person or no person. Never "I", "me" or "my" unless the topic says lived is true.
- No numbers, prices or facts that aren't in the slides. No em dashes, no emoji, no "link in bio", no "follow for more", no "comment below", no "tag a friend".
- The whole caption reads like one person typed it, not a brand.
