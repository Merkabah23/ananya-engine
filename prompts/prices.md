## What things cost now (2026, big Indian cities; the reader is a salaried 25-35 year old)
Use these levels for every everyday price. They are texture, framed in "you" or as typical, never stated as data.
When a reaction is the point (a parent's shock, a friend's envy, a guilty lie), the price must be one that really gets that reaction today. When unsure, take the higher, current number; an old or sale-rack price makes the reader think "who pays that?" and the post loses them.

- Your pay: ₹8-25 lakh a year, ₹60,000-1,60,000 a month in hand. A first job: ₹35,000-60,000 a month.
- Rent, 2BHK: ₹35,000-80,000 a month (more in Mumbai and Bengaluru). A flat to buy: ₹1-3 crore. EMI on it: ₹80,000-2,00,000.
